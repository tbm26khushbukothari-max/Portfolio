# Brand assets

Store any brand assets here that you own the rights to use.

Examples:
- ROOS India logo, lookbook images, brand guidelines
- RuSticks brand assets, product photography
- Award certificates or badges (E4M Silver, ET Digiplus Bronze, Global Gold)
- Press clippings or publication logos (if permitted)
- Your own photography from events (Khar Gymkhana, Lil Flea, Masters' Union)

Do NOT store brand assets belonging to clients (Nykaa, Dabur, Colgate, Myntra, H&M, etc.)
without explicit written permission. Use your own work photography instead.
