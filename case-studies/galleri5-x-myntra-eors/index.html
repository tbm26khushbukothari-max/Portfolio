Context

Myntra's End of Reason Sale — India's largest fashion commerce event.

The End of Reason Sale (EORS) is Myntra's flagship biannual sale — a multi-day event where 20+ lifestyle and apparel brands simultaneously push aggressive product and discount messaging across every digital channel. The creative demand during EORS is enormous: each brand needs dozens of size variants, format variants, and SKU-specific assets refreshed daily.

Galleri5 is an AI-powered image generation platform purpose-built for fashion commerce. I led the campaign strategy and execution for the Myntra EORS partnership, working across brand teams and Galleri5's product to deploy AI-generated creative at a scale that conventional production could not reach.

Problem

The creative bottleneck that every sale breaks against.

Fashion brands running EORS typically need 500–1,000 creative assets across a 10-day window — different sizes for Instagram, Google Display, Myntra banners, and push notifications, all with updated pricing, offers, and product swaps as inventory shifts daily. Traditional production teams take 3–5 days per creative batch. At sale pace, that's a structural impossibility.

The brief was to prove that AI image generation could compress the creative cycle from days to hours without sacrificing the visual quality that premium fashion brands require.

Approach

Build an AI-native creative pipeline, not an AI-assisted one.

The distinction mattered. AI-assisted means a human produces a brief and a human refines the output. AI-native means the pipeline is designed so that brand inputs (product images, offer copy, brand guidelines) flow in and production-ready assets flow out — with human review at the end, not throughout.

I worked with Galleri5's product team to configure brand-specific models for H&M, Cultfit, and 18 other participating labels, each trained on brand guidelines to ensure output consistency. The campaign architecture ran concurrent asset batches by brand by day — so as Day 3 assets were being reviewed, Day 4 generation was already running.

"The constraint is not creativity. It is throughput. AI removes the throughput ceiling and lets creative decisions be made at the speed of commerce."

Framing that shaped how we pitched the Myntra partnership
Execution

30 days, 20 brands, zero production delays.

I managed the brand relationship layer — briefing timelines, reviewing outputs against brand standards, and coordinating last-minute offer changes that required same-day asset regeneration. The EORS window ran across two bursts (pre-sale and sale week), with a total of 100+ discrete campaigns and over 50,000 creative activations across formats and channels.

H&M and Cultfit were the highest-volume accounts. H&M required multilingual copy variants across Hindi and English. Cultfit needed daily refresh tied to flash offers that changed every 24 hours. Both would have been operationally impossible at this turnaround without the AI pipeline.

Impact

Global Gold recognition. A replicable model for AI-native campaign production.

The EORS partnership delivered 50,000+ activations across 100+ campaigns for 20+ brands without a single production delay. The campaign won a Global Gold Award for scale and execution quality — the highest recognition in the category.

Beyond the award: the Galleri5 model demonstrated that AI-native creative production is not a future state but a present capability. The pipeline architecture we built during EORS became the template Galleri5 used for subsequent brand partnerships.

Learnings

Speed reveals the weaknesses in your brief, not your production.

The biggest friction point was not the AI generation — it was getting sharp, decision-ready briefs from brand teams who were used to having days of production time to refine their thinking. When output is instantaneous, vague input becomes the bottleneck. The teams that had their brief dialled — clear product, clear offer, clear tone — saw the best results. The ones who used production time as thinking time struggled.

Lesson: AI-native production requires brief quality that traditional timelines can mask. That is a discipline problem, not a technology one.
