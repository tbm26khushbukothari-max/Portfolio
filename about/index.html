About

I am a brand strategist who learned the work by doing it.

I started ROOS India out of undergrad, joined a cultural-platform founder's office to help it raise $5M, led activations at Galleri5 that earned a Global Gold, and most recently owned Rs 6 Cr+ in agency accounts at Tonic Worldwide across Nykaa, Dabur, and Colgate.

At Masters' Union, I am closing the loop on the technology side. I build with AI daily: Google AI Studio for content prototyping, KNIME and Tableau for data analysis, Claude Code for product builds, Whisper Flow for research workflows, and n8n for automation. The goal is not to seem technical. It is to make brand decisions that are defensible when a PM asks why.

What I am building toward: a role where brand insight and product thinking sit at the same table, at a company that operates at global scale and measures everything.
