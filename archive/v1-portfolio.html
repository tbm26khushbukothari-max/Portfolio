National Award · Silver E4M Brand Campaign 2024

Making a heritage herbal toothpaste irresistible to Gen Z

As Content and Strategy Lead at Tonic Worldwide, I led the cross-functional team that conceived and shipped the Dabur Herb'l x Star Wars campaign, turning a legacy oral-care brand into a pop-culture moment.

Problem
A heritage herbal toothpaste with declining relevance among 18 to 28 urban consumers who did not see Ayurvedic as a desirable identity signal.
Approach
Bridged the brand's herbal identity with the Star Wars IP through a story-first content system anchored on the "force of nature" insight, deployed across digital, social, and creator partnerships. Used AI-assisted content tooling to accelerate ideation and visual production.
Impact
Two national awards, a measurable lift in brand consideration with the 18 to 28 cohort, and a replicable playbook the agency now uses for heritage-brand reinventions.

Founder · 0 to 1 D2C · Sustainability 2019–2020

Founding a sustainable label that put 1,000+ women back in their own wardrobes

I built ROOS India, an upcycled fashion label, straight out of my undergrad, growing it into a pan-India D2C operation showcased at Khar Gymkhana and organically onboarded to Etsy at zero CAC.

Problem
India produces enormous quantities of textile waste. Consumers want sustainable options but not the aesthetic compromise that typically comes with upcycled goods.
Approach
Built a D2C operation around desirability-first upcycling: sourcing unused garments from customers, reworking them into contemporary silhouettes, and building the brand story around reclaiming identity rather than reducing waste.
Impact
Rs 5L+ revenue from a standing start, 500+ garments sold pan-India, and a community of 1,000+ women repurposing what was once forgotten.

About

Brand thinking. Product instinct. AI tools.

I am a brand strategist who learned the work by doing it. I started ROOS India out of undergrad, joined a cultural-platform founder's office to help it raise $5M, led activations at Galleri5 that earned a Global Gold, and most recently owned Rs 6 Cr+ in agency accounts at Tonic Worldwide across Nykaa, Dabur, and Colgate.

At Masters' Union, I am closing the loop on the technology side. I build with AI daily: Google AI Studio for content prototyping, KNIME and Tableau for data analysis, Claude Code for product builds, Whisper Flow for research workflows, and n8n for automation. The goal is not to seem technical. It is to make brand decisions that are defensible when a PM asks why.

What I am building toward: a role where brand insight and product thinking sit at the same table, at a company that operates at global scale and measures everything.

01
Start with the consumer.

The cleanest deck loses to the team that actually understands who is on the other side of the screen.

02
Ship, then sharpen.

A live MVP teaches more in a week than a strategy document does in a quarter.

03
Let data kill the ego.

The best brand instinct is still a hypothesis. Dashboards exist to tell you when you are wrong, and I read them.
